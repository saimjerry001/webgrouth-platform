# Web Grouth USA & UK Off-Page SEO Campaign

## Goal

Build Web Grouth's authority in the USA and UK through real business profiles, expert contributions, useful resources, and relevant editorial mentions. Success is measured by qualified enquiries, branded searches, referral visits, and earned links—not by link count alone.

## Safety rules

- No automated link blasts, private blog network links, fake profiles, or fake reviews.
- Do not pay specifically for a ranking-passing link. Paid placements must be disclosed and use the appropriate link qualification.
- Use natural brand or descriptive anchor text. Do not repeatedly force the same commercial keyword.
- Do not claim results, customer totals, or database statistics that cannot be verified.
- No outreach is sent until the target, sender identity, and final message are approved.

## Phase 2: Linkable asset

### Asset to create now

**USA & UK Guest Post Quality Checklist**

This practical resource will help a buyer check a publisher before ordering a guest post. It can become a dedicated website page and a downloadable one-page checklist.

The checklist should cover:

1. Audience country matches the campaign market.
2. The website has real and recent organic traffic.
3. The niche and article topic are relevant.
4. Recent posts are indexed in Google.
5. Outbound links are not excessive or obviously spammy.
6. The publisher clearly confirms link type, TAT, permanence, and editorial rules.
7. The article is useful and written for readers—not built around an unnatural anchor.
8. The live page is checked after publishing and monitored for removal.

Primary internal destination: `/guest-posting`

Suggested title: **Guest Post Quality Checklist for USA & UK Campaigns**

Suggested description: **Use this simple checklist to review traffic, relevance, indexation, link terms, and content quality before you order a guest post.**

### Future data-led asset

**USA & UK Guest Posting Benchmark 2026**

This asset should only be produced from real Web Grouth inventory data. A CSV export is needed with these fields where available:

- Domain or anonymized domain ID
- Primary niche
- Main audience country
- DR and/or DA
- Estimated organic traffic
- Typical turnaround time
- Link type
- Indexation status
- Replacement or permanence terms
- Publisher price band

Domains and confidential prices may be anonymized. No benchmark statistic will be published until the source data is checked.

## Measurement

- Branded searches for Web Grouth
- Referral visits from earned profiles and publications
- USA and UK organic clicks

- New referring domains that are relevant and indexed
- Rankings and conversions for the service pages—not only homepage rankings
