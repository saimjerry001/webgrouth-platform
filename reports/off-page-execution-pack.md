## Founder contribution outline

Working title: What an agency should confirm before approving a guest post website

1. One real situation where the initial shortlist did not fit the brief.
2. Evidence actually reviewed: audience country, topic, recent publication quality and written terms.
3. What changed after the review, without claiming unmeasured ranking effects.
4. A short reader checklist drawn from that experience.

Required evidence: a permitted, anonymized brief; dates; the approved terms; and a real outcome. Do not invent a case study, traffic uplift or client quote.
