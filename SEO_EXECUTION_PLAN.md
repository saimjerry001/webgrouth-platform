# Web Grouth SEO Execution Plan

Target markets: United States and United Kingdom  
Primary conversion: qualified guest post, link insertion and off-page SEO enquiries  
Baseline date: 2 September 2026

## Target-page map

| Page | Main search intent | Supporting themes |
| --- | --- | --- |

| `/guest-posting` | Guest posting services for USA and UK | editorial guest posts, niche guest posting, bulk guest posts |

Use the phrases as topics, not as repeated exact-match wording. Copy must stay clear and natural.

## Technical and on-page foundation

- Use `https://www.webgrouth.com` as the only canonical host.
- Keep one unique title, description and H1 on every indexable page.
- Keep form-success and 404 pages out of the search index.
- Generate the XML sitemap from the real page and blog collections.
- Keep Organization, WebSite, Service, FAQ, BlogPosting and breadcrumb data consistent with visible content.
- Link useful guides to the relevant service page with descriptive, natural anchor text.
- Submit `/sitemap.xml` in Google Search Console and inspect the four commercial pages after release.

## 90-day off-page campaign

### Weeks 1–2: measurement and brand trust

1. Verify the `www` domain property in Google Search Console and connect analytics.
2. Record the starting position for each target page: clicks, impressions, indexed status, referring domains and USA/UK enquiries.
3. Complete real company profiles on relevant business and agency platforms. Use the same brand name, description, domain and contact details everywhere.
4. Find unlinked mentions of Web Grouth and request a link only where it helps the reader.

### Weeks 3–6: useful editorial coverage

1. Publish one original data-led resource using Web Grouth's publisher-network experience, such as a USA/UK publisher selection checklist or turnaround benchmark.
2. Pitch relevant SEO, SaaS, marketing and agency publications with a useful angle written for their audience.
3. Link to the page that genuinely supports the article. Use brand or descriptive wording that reads naturally in the sentence.
4. Avoid automated links, irrelevant directory blasts, sitewide links and repeated keyword-heavy anchors.
5. Mark advertising or paid placements with the appropriate `rel="sponsored"` or `nofollow` attribute.

### Weeks 7–10: authority assets and partnerships

1. Publish one detailed client case study after obtaining permission and confirming the results.
2. Build partner citations with real SEO agencies, writers and publishers where a commercial relationship already exists.
3. Offer expert comments or first-party publisher data to journalists and industry writers covering link building and content marketing.
4. Reclaim relevant broken links only when Web Grouth has a genuinely suitable replacement resource.

### Weeks 11–13: review and scale

1. Compare referring-domain relevance, USA/UK organic visibility and qualified leads against the baseline.
2. Refresh pages receiving impressions but weak click-through rates.
3. Strengthen internal links to pages moving onto page two or the lower half of page one.
4. Repeat only the outreach formats that produced relevant visits, enquiries or credible editorial mentions.

## Quality rules

- Relevance and editorial value come before DA or DR.
- Every link should make sense without relying on exact-match keywords.
- No fabricated reviews, traffic claims, case studies or client names.
- No ranking guarantees.
- Paid promotion must be disclosed and correctly qualified.
- Measure qualified enquiries and useful USA/UK visibility, not link count alone.

## Access needed for measurement and execution

- Google Search Console access for `https://www.webgrouth.com/` or the domain property.
- Analytics access, if installed.
- Approved client results that can become case studies.
- Permission boundaries for using the existing publisher database in Web Grouth's own outreach campaign.
